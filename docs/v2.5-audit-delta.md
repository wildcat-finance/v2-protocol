# v2.5 Consolidated Pre-Audit Delta

Baseline: branch `main` at `c7be403` (the deployed, audited v2 protocol).
Candidate source snapshot: `feat/v2.5-hooks-role-providers` at `822045f`.
Scope: `git diff c7be403...822045f -- src/` - 59 files, 5,856 insertions and 425 deletions over 116 commits.

This document consolidates three independent review streams run over the full
delta as a single artifact: two per-subsystem correctness reviews (codex), one
cross-change interaction review (multi-agent workflow, 31 agents), plus a
mechanical-proof pass over the resulting fixes. It is intended as the
auditor-facing inventory of what changed, why, what was found, and what
evidence backs the current state.

## 1. Executive summary

- Five release areas: **revolving credit markets** (WildcatMarketRevolving + HooksFactoryRevolving), **periodic-term hooks** (recurring withdrawal windows + proposed/permissionless APR reductions), a **lens rework** (facade + helper contracts, with a v2.5 ABI that includes the periodic pending-APR-reduction execution flag), **borrower identity and transfer** (operational borrower/principal separation, account registry, two-step market transfer, principal-aware sanctions, and transfer-aware wrapper authority), and **hook/provider authority separation** (transferable hook administration, reusable access-list providers, and explicit pull-credential cache policy).
- One deliberate hardening with protocol-wide reach: transfer/deposit/
  withdrawal scaling changed from half-up (`rayDiv`/`scaleAmount`) to
  **floor** (`scaleAmountDown`). Every regression found in this review
  traces to that change composing with exact-boundary consumers; all are
  fixed (section 3) with mechanical proofs and regression pins.
- Remediation series CR-01..CR-07 and the CAF audit responses are folded in
  and tagged per file below.
- **Deployed singletons are untouched**: WildcatArchController,
  WildcatSanctionsSentinel, WildcatSanctionsEscrow, and the SphereX base have
  zero bytecode-affecting changes (independently confirmed twice).
- The 4626 wrapper was rebuilt for floor-rounding markets and its factory is
  now a generation-routing facade; covered by its own review cycle and the
  EIP-4626 audit-scope note.
- The borrower-transfer and hook/provider feature series are implemented and have passed the full test suite and deploy-profile size checks. The prior coverage appendix predates both feature series and must be refreshed before the final audit handoff.

## 3. Findings and resolutions

### 3.1 Fixed, with mechanical proofs and regression pins

**F1 — Batch settlement vs exact-debt close (HIGH).** Floor capacity
(`scaleAmountDown(availableLiquidity)`) under-counted by one scaled token
whenever `frac(S * sf / RAY)` fell in `(0, 0.5)`, making `closeMarket`
revert `CloseMarketWithUnpaidWithdrawals` on fully-funded closes with the
supply queued, and permanently stranding one scaled token of full-withdrawal
batches on already-closed markets (repayment is impossible there). Both
reproduced on-EVM before the fix.
*Fix:* `maxScaledSettleableAmount` — the largest `k` with
`floor(k * sf / RAY) <= n`. Proven (exact-integer search, 40k+ random pairs
plus edge sweeps, zero counterexamples): never pays more than available;
exactly maximal; widens the old capacity by at most one scaled token; and the
close-settlement identity `k_max >= S` holds for all supplies and scale
factors in both rounding branches of the debt normalization. Regression pins:
`test/integration/ProductionMatrixScenarios.t.sol` (with stranding-window
vacuity guards) and the matrix invariant unwind at **zero** stranding
tolerance.

**F2 — Exact-minimum deposits (MEDIUM).** Floor-scaled deposits round-tripped
to `minimumDeposit - 1` wei in the hooks' half-up recomputation, rejecting
exact-minimum tenders on all three templates once any interest accrued.
*Fix:* compare in scaled units. Proven: every tender `>= minimumDeposit` is
accepted at any scale factor; the acceptance band below the minimum is
bounded by one scaled token (worst case ~5.19e6 wei at the maximal uint112
scale factor); tiny-minimum edge backstopped by the market's
`NullMintAmount`. Boundary pinned exactly (smallest accepted tender and
largest rejected tender) across all three templates at accrued scale factors.

### 3.2 Accepted behavior (documented, not fixed)

**F3 — Sanctions quarantine deferred by withdrawal gates.** `nukeFromOrbit`
routes through the ordinary withdrawal path (caf-03 decision), so periodic
windows defer quarantine until the next window — recurring per period, up to
365 days — as a long fixed term already could. Debated and accepted;
documented in the caf-03 comment and Known Issues; pinned by
`test/integration/ProductionMatrixScenarios.t.sol`.

**F4 — Credential wrong-denial vs v2 baseline (LOW).** When hooksData names a
pull provider and the presented credential fails validation, the provider's
`getCredential` is no longer consulted as a fallback. Fail-closed, never
wrong-approval, retryable by resubmitting without the bad hooksData.

### 3.3 Clean surfaces (verified, not just unexamined)

Accounting invariants (scale-factor monotonicity, totalDebts composition,
batch conservation, borrowable bounds); every remaining rounding call site in
core traced with rounding direction judged correct; hook-calling assembly
layouts vs IHooks; the revolving virtual seams under every interleaving
including two-segment batch-expiry accrual; the three templates'
InvalidAccessConfiguration differences (intentional); HooksFactoryRevolving
vs HooksFactory divergences (enumerated, all intentional); interfaces vs
implementations; version-string consumers (`'2.5'` keeps the first-byte
`'2'` contract).

## 4. Accepted behavioral deltas vs the audited v2

1. Transfer/deposit/withdrawal scaling rounds **down** (was half-up); the
   rounding wei always disadvantages the acting party or favors the market.
   Markets declare this via `scaledTransferRounding()`.
2. `version()` returns `'2.5'` (was `'2'`); first byte preserved.
3. Batch payments settle up to the exact floor-priced capacity (F1 fix) —
   strictly more settlement than v2.5-pre-fix, at most one scaled token more
   than the naive floor, never exceeding available liquidity.
4. Minimum-deposit enforcement is scaled-space (F2 fix) with a sub-one-scaled-
   token acceptance band under the nominal minimum.
5. Credential fallback narrowing (F4). 6. Periodic quarantine deferral (F3).
7. Revolving markets accrue `commitmentFee + APR * min(drawn, supply)/supply`
   rather than APR on full supply.
8. Market borrower authority is a storage-backed two-step transfer rather than an immutable deployment identity. Pending status grants no authority, acceptance revalidates identity, registration, and raw sanctions state, and transfer is available in active, delinquent, and closed markets.
9. Markets record operational borrower separately from registered principal. Approved account factories register each account once through the new identity registry. Account principals may transfer their association through a two-step path, while each market continues using its stored accepted identity until that market explicitly accepts the new principal.
10. Lender-facing sanctions checks use the market principal, raw borrower/principal checks gate draws and transfers, and wrapper authority follows live market identity. Hooks and providers do not move implicitly with a market transfer.
11. Hook administration is a separate two-step authority. The target must be an ArchController-registered principal at request and acceptance, the pending administrator has no authority, and the creating factory's association index updates atomically with acceptance.
12. A hook administrator is no longer installed as a permanent push provider. Hook transfer preserves provider attachments, lender state, hook-local blocks, known-lender state, and hooked-market configuration.
13. `AccessListRoleProvider` provides a reusable address list with independent two-step administration. TTL zero makes a pull credential non-cacheable, including within the same block; a positive TTL deliberately accepts delayed removal until the cache window expires.
14. `MerkleRoleProvider` verifies only canonical sorted-pair proofs for `keccak256(abi.encode(account))`. Root and administrator changes preserve the provider address and hook attachments, and the configured hook TTL remains the only credential cache policy.
15. `ERC4626AssetsRoleProvider` grants access from the configured vault's current ideal asset conversion. TTL zero follows live share ownership, positive TTL deliberately caches access, hook-local blocks remain authoritative, and a vault failure yields no credential. A Wildcat wrapper may gate another market but cannot self-reference through its wrapped market's guarded `scaleFactor()`.
16. `ERC20RoleProvider` grants access from the configured token's current reported balance. TTL zero follows live balances, positive TTL deliberately caches access, hook-local blocks remain authoritative, and a token failure yields no credential. A Wildcat market token may gate another market but cannot self-reference through its guarded `balanceOf()`.
17. `ERC721RoleProvider` grants access from any positive balance in the configured collection. TTL zero follows live ownership, positive TTL deliberately caches access, hook-local blocks remain authoritative, and a collection failure yields no credential. The interface-check bypass changes constructor validation only.
18. `ERC1155RoleProvider` grants access from any positive balance of one configured token ID. Other token IDs do not qualify. TTL zero follows the live configured balance, positive TTL deliberately caches access, hook-local blocks remain authoritative, and a collection failure yields no credential. The interface-check bypass changes constructor validation only.
19. `queueWithdrawalScaled` queues an exact caller-owned scaled amount using the execution-time scale factor for its normalized event value. It uses the existing queue hook, accounting, batch, and settlement paths without adding storage, authority, beneficiaries, or events. This lets a Safe or other smart account redeem wrapper shares and queue exactly those shares in one atomic transaction while preserving any direct market-token balance held by the same account.
20. `onExecuteWithdrawal` receives the exact batch expiry used for the claim. The callback still runs before market state is written or assets are transferred, but it no longer has to guess which historical batch produced the payment.
