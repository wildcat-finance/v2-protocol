## Release boundary

V2.0 is the base market generation documented here. Its core surface includes:

- Borrower-operated, isolated lending markets deployed through `HooksFactory`.
- Scaled lender balances, rebasing debt, and batched withdrawals.
- `OpenTermHooks` and `FixedTermHooks` access policy.
- The ArchController registry and sanctions sentinel.
- The original V2 `MarketLens` integration surface.

## Compatibility

V2.0 markets use half-up normalized-to-scaled conversion. They do not expose:

- `scaledTransferRounding()`
- `queueWithdrawalScaled(uint256)`

The tagged source contains one standard market implementation. Later releases
add the canonical ERC-4626 wrapper, revolving markets, periodic-term hooks, and
borrower transfers.
