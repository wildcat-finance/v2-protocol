## Release Boundary

V2.0 is the base market generation documented by this repository. Its core
surface includes:

- borrower-operated, isolated lending markets deployed through `HooksFactory`;
- scaled lender balances, rebasing debt, and batched withdrawals;
- `OpenTermHooks` and `FixedTermHooks` access policy;
- the ArchController registry and sanctions sentinel; and
- the original V2 `MarketLens` integration surface.

## Compatibility

V2.0 markets use the original half-up normalized-to-scaled conversion. They do
not expose `scaledTransferRounding()` or `queueWithdrawalScaled(uint256)`.

The tagged source contains one standard market implementation. It does not
contain the canonical ERC-4626 wrapper, revolving markets, periodic-term hooks,
or borrower transfers added by later releases.
