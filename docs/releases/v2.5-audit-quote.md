## Scope

Review all remaining `src/**/*.sol` files on the audit branch and the prepared
creation-code installation constructors listed above. This includes standard
and revolving markets, accounting and withdrawal settlement, repayment and
default, factories, term hooks, borrower identity, sanctions, SphereX integration,
ERC-4626 wrappers, lenses, libraries, and interfaces.

```text
src/
├── *.sol       # Factories, controller, identity, sanctions, reentrancy
├── market/     # Standard/revolving markets, configuration, withdrawals
├── access/     # Hooks, term policies, access control, provider integration
├── providers/  # AccessList provider, factory, shared base, interfaces
├── vault/      # ERC-4626 wrapper and factory
├── lens/       # Aggregated and live read surfaces
├── libraries/  # Accounting, math, queues, creation-code readers
├── types/      # Hook configuration, credentials, transient data
├── interfaces/ # Shared contract interfaces, events, errors, structs
└── spherex/    # SphereX configuration and protection integration
script/common/PreparedInitCodeStorage.sol  # Installation constructors
```

Role-provider scope is `AccessListRoleProvider.sol`,
`AccessListRoleProviderFactory.sol`, the shared `ManagedRoleProvider.sol` base,
and their interfaces. Other provider implementations and their dedicated
factories/interfaces are excluded and
removed from the audit branch. Shared provider interfaces, hook integration,
and lens behavior remain in scope. The ERC-4626 wrapper is included; the
ERC-4626-assets credential provider is excluded.

Third-party library implementations under `lib/` are excluded from a standalone
dependency audit; Wildcat's use of them and its external-call boundaries remain
in scope. Tests and documentation support the review. Deployment tooling and
ceremonies, address verification, SDK, frontend, subgraph, keepers, and external
sanctions/SphereX services are outside this quote.

## Context for reviewers

Wildcat provides undercollateralized credit. Borrower default and actions within
documented borrower authority are credit risk; broken accounting or exceeded
authority are security concerns. Review priorities are:

- Scaled balances, interest, revolving commitment fees, rounding, and withdrawal
  allocation; repayment deadlines, default records, closure, and surplus.
- Hook and provider permissions, borrower transfers, callback failures and
  reentrancy; deterministic deployment and creation-code integrity.
- Wrapper share backing, generation routing, recipient policy, sanctions escrow,
  and agreement between lens results and executable behavior.

Underlying lending assets are expected to have conventional, stable ERC-20
behavior. Market tokens rebase; wrapper shares do not. There is no price oracle,
AMM, bridge, or cross-chain messaging. Sanctions checks depend on an external
list. Intended targets are Ethereum and Plasma, with independent deployments
requiring Cancun/EIP-1153 support.

The [technical scope](https://github.com/wildcat-finance/v2-protocol/blob/529f2db55d656b89db4b17ecf6685b7589d5d7dd/docs/releases/v2.5-audit-scope.md),
[security assumptions](https://github.com/wildcat-finance/v2-protocol/blob/529f2db55d656b89db4b17ecf6685b7589d5d7dd/docs/security/assumptions.md), and
[known limitations](https://github.com/wildcat-finance/v2-protocol/blob/529f2db55d656b89db4b17ecf6685b7589d5d7dd/docs/security/known-issues.md)
supply the detailed boundaries.
Prior reviews are indexed in [`audits/`](https://github.com/wildcat-finance/v2-protocol/blob/529f2db55d656b89db4b17ecf6685b7589d5d7dd/audits/README.md). This review
