# V2.5

## Source boundary

This page describes V2.5's contract and interface changes from
[V2.1](./v2.1.md). Network addresses and deployed versions are recorded in
[`deployments/`](../../deployments/).

The retained [review material](./README.md#v25-review-material) covers earlier
pinned source. The [audit scope](./v2.5-audit-scope.md) identifies the subsequent
changes and requirements for reviewing a new source freeze.

## Markets and accounting

- `WildcatMarketRevolving` adds revolving-credit accounting with separate drawn
  principal and commitment-fee accrual.
- Normalized amounts entering scaled accounting now round down. Markets expose
  `version() == "2.5"` and `scaledTransferRounding()` for compatibility checks.
- `queueWithdrawalScaled(uint256)` preserves an exact scaled position for
  wrapper and account-abstraction flows.
- Withdrawal settlement uses floor-priced capacity so a fully funded closed
  market can finish its batches.
- Partial withdrawal payments retain their fractional value between payments.
  Debt and reserves include those non-interest-bearing fractions. Cumulative
  scaled batch ownership remains capped at `uint104.max` despite the `uint128`
  ABI fields; payment also respects the global unclaimed-withdrawal capacity.
- New markets expose optional immutable repayment terms. From the date they
  require 100% reserves, charge immediate underfunded penalty, reject deposits
  and borrowing, and bypass queue-hook restrictions while preserving batches.
- Every new market records a permanent default timestamp after an uncured
  90-day penalty run or missed inclusive repayment deadline. Default itself
  changes no market permissions or accounting.
- Scheduled markets close automatically once fully funded, without a closure
  hook veto or borrower surplus transfer. The borrower collects surplus
  separately after closure, and old withdrawal batches can finish in bounded
  calls.
- Withdrawal collection no longer calls `onExecuteWithdrawal`. New markets
  reject hook configurations that enable that callback; market-level sanctions
  handling remains in place.

See [accounting](../protocol/accounting.md),
[repayment and default](../protocol/repayment-and-default.md),
[scaling](../protocol/scaling-and-rounding.md), and
[withdrawals](../protocol/withdrawals.md).

## Terms, identity, and access

- `PeriodicTermHooks` adds recurring withdrawal windows and announced APR
  reductions.
- The three hook templates reuse `BaseHooks` and separate term policies.
  Additional source policies compose through explicit extension points;
  competing decisions require an explicit override in the concrete hook.
- Market borrower and registered principal are stored separately, with a
  two-step borrower-transfer path. Supported V2.5 origination keeps them equal.
- Hook administration is separate from market borrower and credential-provider
  authority.
- Credential validation rejects future push timestamps, classifies non-pull
  providers defensively, and avoids repeated provider queries.
- Access configurations cannot allow an uncredentialed entry path while gating
  the same holder's withdrawal path.

See [borrower identity](../protocol/borrower-identity.md),
[hook development](../integrations/hook-development.md),
[access control](../integrations/access-control.md), and
[periodic-term hooks](../integrations/periodic-term-hooks.md).

## Wrappers and read surfaces

- `Wildcat4626WrapperFactory` is a generation-aware facade. It handles V2.5
  floor-rounding markets locally and forwards compatible earlier markets to the
  V2.1 factory.
- V2.5 markets atomically register their canonical wrapper and prevent the
  wrapper's pooled position from being quarantined at market level.
- Built-in access hooks admit that registered wrapper as a market-token
  recipient whenever transfers are enabled, without extending the exemption to
  noncanonical wrappers or unwrap receivers.
- `MarketLens` is a facade over core, aggregation, and live-data helpers to
  remain within deployment-size limits.
- Lens tuples and factory deployment events add borrower-principal, hook,
  provider, fee, and revolving-market state.
- Full and live V2.5 lens tuples expose repayment terms, recorded default,
  deposit/borrow capacity, total debts, and closed-market recoverable surplus.
  Hook metadata includes repayment bounds, periodic proposals, and registered
  creation-code commitments. These additions require updated return decoders.
- Shared hook metadata also changes the common `getMarketData` return tuple
  and results containing it. Regenerate lens bindings for V2.0/V2.1 reads
  through the new lens as well as for V2.5 reads.
- Consumers must select event and lens ABIs from deployment provenance rather
  than version text or address age.
- Lens token names and symbols use bounded, best-effort reads. Decimals and
  required accounting remain strict. Factory metadata decoding accepts
  canonical empty labels without relaxing length or denomination checks.

See [market lenses](../integrations/lenses.md),
[ERC-4626 wrapper](../integrations/erc-4626-wrapper.md), and
[event model](../integrations/events.md).

## Deployment boundary

V2.5 adds:

- New market factories and stored market initcode.
- Hook-template initcode and the revolving market family.
- The generation-aware wrapper-factory facade.
- A borrower identity registry and access-list provider factory.
- The lens facade.

Oversized creation artifacts use two immutable storage contracts behind one
factory-facing address. Fitting hooks retain raw storage. Both factories check
the original artifact hash before deployment; hook-template registration now
requires that immutable commitment. Deployment plans, resume verification and
handoffs track both split contracts.

Factories skip origination-fee token calls when the fee is zero. Token and
amount matching remain mandatory; positive fees still require a transfer.

Builds use optimizer runs `1` and the exact Yul sequence in
[`foundry.toml`](../../foundry.toml). Verification must reproduce the complete
compiler configuration. See [deployment](../operations/deployment.md).

Exact deployed generations and lifecycle state belong to
[`deployments/`](../../deployments/).

The release plan reuses the existing `WildcatArchController`,
`WildcatSanctionsSentinel`, and `WildcatSanctionsEscrow`. It does not deploy new
compilations of those files.

The active branch still carries compiler, comment, and sanctions-query
maintenance in singleton source. Auditors must distinguish the repository diff
from the runtime deployment scope.

Source ancestry alone does not show which factories, templates, providers, or
helpers are live.

## Compatibility

Pre-V2.5 markets keep half-up scaled conversion. They do not expose the new
scaled-withdrawal or borrower-transfer interfaces. Existing hook instances keep
their deployed code and configuration.

Integrations spanning generations must resolve the market factory, hook
template, wrapper factory, rounding marker, event ABI, and lens ABI from the
recorded deployment generation.

The appended repayment fields change factory market-input tuples and
`IHooks.onCreateMarket`. Parameter-constraint results grow from ten to twelve
words, `addHooksTemplate` gains an artifact-hash argument, and nested lens
return tuples change. Regenerate callers and decoders from the selected
deployment ABI, and index the repayment/default and template-commitment events.

Carry accounting extends `MarketState` from fourteen to fifteen words and
`WithdrawalBatch` from three to four. Getter selectors stay the same, but hook
callbacks carrying `MarketState` have new selectors. Use matching market/hook
artifacts and carry-aware lens, SDK, and indexer accounting. Unchanged withdrawal
events do not expose the added fractions. See
[accounting tuple compatibility](../integrations/lenses.md#accounting-tuple-compatibility).

This release provides the market lifecycle and hook extension surfaces for
later features. It does not include a tranche vault, senior/junior shares,
waterfall, or tranche policy; those remain V2.6 work.

Review [known limitations and accepted behavior](../security/known-issues.md)
before evaluating or operating a V2.5 market.
